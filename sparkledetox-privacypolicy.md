> **Short version:** SparkleDetox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## 1. Overview

SparkleDetox ("the app") is a minimalist Android launcher designed to reduce screen time by adding intentional friction to app access. This privacy policy describes how SparkleApp Labs handles information in connection with the app.

## 2. Information we do not collect

SparkleDetox does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. Specifically, the app does not collect:

- Your name, email address, or any contact information
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers
- Location data
- Usage statistics or analytics
- App usage patterns or screen time data
- Crash reports or diagnostic data sent off-device
- Any other personally identifiable information

## 3. Data stored on your device

SparkleDetox stores the following data locally on your device only, and this data never leaves your device:

- App configuration preferences (e.g., cooldown timer settings, launcher preferences)
- Usage history used to generate on-device reports within the app

This data is stored in Android's standard local storage and is removed when you uninstall the app. It is never transmitted to SparkleApp Labs or any third party.

## 4. Third-party services

SparkleDetox does not integrate with any third-party analytics platforms, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or cloud services. No third-party SDKs that collect data are included in the app.

## 5. Permissions

SparkleDetox may request certain Android permissions to function as a launcher. Any permissions requested are used solely to provide core app functionality on your device and are not used to collect or transmit data. You can review and manage permissions at any time in your device's Settings.

## 6. Children's privacy

SparkleDetox does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it does not present privacy risks specific to children.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If SparkleApp Labs makes material changes to this privacy policy — for example if a future version of the app introduces optional data features — this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Continued use of the app after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
